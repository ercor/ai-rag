# Q3 2026 Company Results

## Financial Summary

Q3 2026 showed strong growth across all business units. Revenue reached $450M, up 15% year-over-year. Operating margin improved to 22%, driven by efficiency gains in cloud infrastructure.

## Key Metrics

- Monthly Active Users: 2.3M (up 18% YoY)
- Customer Retention Rate: 94%
- Net Promoter Score: 72
- Employee Satisfaction: 4.2/5.0

## Product Highlights

The RAG platform launch exceeded expectations with 500 enterprise customers in the first quarter. The AI-powered search feature reduced average query resolution time by 40%.

## Regional Performance

- North America: $250M revenue (56% of total)
- Europe: $130M revenue (29% of total)
- Asia Pacific: $70M revenue (15% of total)

## Outlook for Q4

We project Q4 revenue of $480-500M, driven by seasonal demand and new enterprise contracts. Key initiatives include expanding the RAG platform to support multilingual queries and launching the mobile SDK.

## Risks and Challenges

Cloud infrastructure costs remain a concern, increasing 12% quarter-over-quarter. The team is evaluating reserved instance commitments and workload optimization to reduce costs by 15% in Q4.
