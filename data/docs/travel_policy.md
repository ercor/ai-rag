# Corporate Travel Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the corporate travel policy for all employees and contractors of Acme Corporation. All travel must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager and the department budget holder.

## Booking Requirements

All travel bookings must be made through the approved corporate travel portal. Employees should book at least 14 days in advance to receive preferred rates. Economy class is the standard for domestic flights. Business class may be approved for international flights exceeding 6 hours.

## Expense Reporting

Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. The finance department will process reimbursements within 5 business days of approved submission.

## Per Diem Rates

Domestic travel per diem: $75 per day for meals. International travel per diem varies by country and is published on the intranet. Hotel accommodations are capped at $200 per night for domestic and $350 for international travel.

## Ground Transportation

Ride-sharing services (Uber, Lyft) are approved for travel between airports and hotels. Rental cars require manager approval and must be booked through the corporate portal. Public transportation is encouraged where available and safe.

## International Travel

International travel requires additional approval from the VP of the division. Passport and visa requirements are the responsibility of the traveler. Travel insurance is provided automatically through the corporate travel portal.

## Policy Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in denial of expense reimbursement. Repeated violations may lead to disciplinary action. Questions about this policy should be directed to the HR department.
